# Common glossary - English to Bulgarian

The words of every course site: the parts of a course, the labels of the pages and
the buttons. They do not depend on the topic of the course.

The terms of one course (its programming language, its tools, its business domain)
are in that course's `i18n/glossary.md`. Where the two differ, the course's glossary
wins.

## The rule for technical terms

**A technical term stays in English** when the Bulgarian word would hide the English
one the readers meet in documentation, error messages and code. A term that is the
same word in both languages is written in Bulgarian (функция, обект, метод,
оператор). Everyday words are translated (стойност, условие, грешка).

Each course decides its own terms with this rule, in its `glossary.md`: the same
English word may stay in English in one course and be translated in another (it
depends on what the readers already know).

A term that stays in English is written in Latin letters, without quotes or
backticks, with the English plural ("strings"). Avoid a Bulgarian article where you
can ("в този string"); where you cannot, add it with a hyphen: "string-ът",
"repository-то".

## Stays in English

| Term | Note |
|---|---|
| Run, Edit, Reset, Present, OK, FAIL | buttons and output of the site and the tasks |
| Homework, Labs (the repositories) | "Homework repository", "Labs repository" |
| commit, push, pull, branch, pull request | verbs: "направете commit", "качете с push" |
| `your code` | the marker in the task files |
